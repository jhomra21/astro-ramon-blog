audiocn Solid ports [audiocn/ui](https://github.com/audiocn/ui) to Solid. Like shadcn, the CLI copies component source into your project, and you edit that copy. The repository has 23 components, 6 blocks, and 18 hooks.

## What it includes

- Meters and visualizers: a level meter with peak, RMS, peak hold, and clip detection, plus spectrum, waveform, and bar visualizers.
- Controls: a decibel fader, knobs, a parameter slider, and a volume control.
- Mixing: channel strips and a mixer with shared meter settings and keyboard navigation.
- Playback: an audio player, a track list, and sound pads with hotkeys.
- Blocks: a system audio mixer, a music player, a soundboard, and a microphone setup.

## Components receive data

The components never open a microphone. They take levels and values as props. The Web Audio hooks handle capture: `useMicrophone` opens a `MediaStream`, `useSystemAudio` captures tab or system audio through the browser picker, and `useAudioAnalyser` reads streams, media elements, or audio nodes. You can also feed the components from another audio engine.

## Solid 1 and Solid 2

The Solid 1 registry targets Solid 1.9.15 or later and is stable. The Solid 2 registry is experimental and targets a Solid 2 release candidate. The two registries use different JSX transforms, so configure the matching runtime before you add components.
